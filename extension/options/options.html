Frequently asked questions

Does KhmerLens work offline?

Yes. All 21,514 words are built into the extension, so lookups happen entirely on your device with no internet connection. The only feature that ever touches the network is the optional kheng.info lookup, which is off by default.

What data does KhmerLens collect?

None. There is no analytics, no tracking, and no network requests. Your settings are stored locally on your device, and the words you look up are never recorded or sent anywhere.

Why isn't a word being found or highlighted?

Khmer has no spaces between words, so KhmerLens segments text using its dictionary. If a word isn't recognized it may be spelled unusually, be a name, or fall outside the bundled dictionary. Press Shift to cycle to shorter or alternative matches under the cursor.

How do I change the Alt+K shortcut?

Open chrome://extensions/shortcuts in your address bar and rebind “Toggle KhmerLens on the current tab” to any key combination you prefer.

Why won't the pronunciation play?

KhmerLens plays a bundled recording when one exists for that word. If there's no recording, it falls back to your system's Khmer text-to-speech voice — which only works if a Khmer voice is installed on your device. Press S to play.

How do I add words to Anki?

Enable Anki integration on the Settings tab. It needs the Anki desktop app running with the free AnkiConnect add-on. Once connected, pick a deck and note type, then press A (or the ★ button) in the popup to add the current word.

What is the kheng.info lookup?

An opt-in fallback for words the offline dictionary doesn't cover. When enabled, pressing K (or the lookup button) sends only that single word to kheng.info to fetch a definition. Nothing is sent unless you ask for it.

Why is kheng.info needed at all?

The bundled dictionary is built only from openly-licensed sources, so rarer words fall outside it. kheng.info has much broader coverage but isn't open source, so its data can't be shipped inside the extension — the optional, on-demand lookup is the way to reach it.

Quick Start

Four short steps to get reading Khmer with KhmerLens. Come back any time.

Turn KhmerLens on

KhmerLens stays off until you switch it on for a tab — nothing runs in the background.

  1. Click the KhmerLens icon in your browser toolbar to open the popup.
  2. Flip the switch to On — or just press AltK to toggle the tab directly.
  3. The toolbar badge reads ON while it's active on that tab.

Hover to look up a word

No clicking or typing — the definition follows your cursor.

  1. Move your cursor over any Khmer text on the page.
  2. A popup appears with the word, romanization, part of speech, and English meaning.
  3. Press Shift to cycle alternative matches, or N / B to step to the next or previous word.

Style the popup

Set the theme, size, and whether romanization shows — with a live preview.

  1. Choose a light, dark, or automatic theme, and small / medium / large text.
  2. Toggle romanization and on-page highlighting to taste.
  3. The preview updates instantly, and every change saves on its own.

Optional extras

Turn these on only if you want them — all off by default.

  • Anki flashcards — connect the Anki desktop app and press A to save words as notes.
  • Pronunciation — press S to hear a word (bundled recording, or your system's Khmer voice).
  • Online lookup — enable kheng.info to fetch definitions for words not in the offline dictionary (press K).
